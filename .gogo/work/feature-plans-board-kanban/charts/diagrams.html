Intended control flow — m move dispatch, spawn + auto-pickup are launched claude -p

  M -->|ready| GO["planGo - fan-out spawn"]
  M -->|active| UAT["planAcceptUAT - project-UAT gate"]
  M -->|done| BOUNCE["terminal - status bounce"]

  GO --> LP["launch claude -p /gogo:plan <brief> --correlation plan-XXXX

  SK --> WB["work board: card appears in its plan phase"]
  GO --> REC["record member + SetStatus active
(ONLY on a successful launch)"]

  UAT --> MSHIP{"every member work item shipped?"}
  MSHIP -->|no| REFUSE["refuse - name unshipped members"]
  MSHIP -->|yes| DONE["MarkDone: append Project UAT round + done

plan STAYS in store as done"]

  WB -. "member at plan-accepted + source planAcceptanceSkip (Phase C, on reload)" .-> CAP{"source under ConcurrentWorkItems cap?"}

ALWAYS a launch, NEVER a CLI state-flip"]
  CAP -->|"at cap"| CUE["needs-manual-trigger cue on BOTH cards
(transient - auto-fires when a slot frees)"]
